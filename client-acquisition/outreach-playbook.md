# PTA Client Acquisition Playbook

## 30-Day Target

- 150 qualified prospects researched.
- 100 tailored outreach messages sent.
- 10 serious conversations booked.
- 3 proposals issued.
- 1 paid compliance or advisory engagement closed.

## Primary Wedge

PTA helps foreign-owned companies in Ghana identify TTA/GIPC exposure before remittance, tax, registration, or renewal issues create delays.

## First Buyer

Start with foreign investors and foreign-owned Ghana companies. Treat law firms, accountants, company secretaries, and investment consultants as referral partners.

## Lead Sources

- GIPC investment reports: identify sectors, foreign investor activity, and parent-country signals.
- GHANEPS/PPA procurement activity: identify companies bidding for public contracts or supplying regulated entities.
- Bank of Ghana regulated institution lists: prioritize PSPs, remittance businesses, digital lenders, forex operators, and other regulated finance companies.
- Foreign chambers and embassies: prioritize UK, EU, US, India, China, Turkey, South Africa, and Nigeria business networks.
- LinkedIn manual search: find country managers, CFOs, compliance heads, legal heads, and founders.

## Priority Sectors

- Telecoms.
- Fintech, PSPs, remittance, digital lending.
- Software/SaaS and platform companies.
- Manufacturing.
- Pharmaceuticals and medical devices.
- Energy and engineering.
- Franchising and branded service businesses.

## Daily 2-Hour Cadence

- 60 minutes: research 15 prospects and add them to `prospect-tracker.csv`.
- 40 minutes: send 10 tailored outreach messages.
- 20 minutes: follow up, update statuses, and schedule next actions.
