# PTA First 150 Lead Build

## Allocation

- 40 leads: GIPC investment-report sectors and foreign investor signals.
- 30 leads: Bank of Ghana regulated finance, PSP, remittance, digital lending, and forex businesses.
- 25 leads: GHANEPS/PPA procurement-heavy suppliers or public-facing technology vendors.
- 25 leads: foreign chambers, embassies, and business councils.
- 30 leads: LinkedIn manual search across priority sectors.

## Qualification Rules

A prospect is qualified only if at least one money signal is present:

- Foreign parent or foreign ownership.
- Ghana subsidiary or active Ghana market entry.
- Cross-border royalty, software, management, technical service, franchise, or IP fee likelihood.
- Regulated industry status.
- Public procurement activity.
- Expansion, hiring, licensing, or investment signal.

## Decision-Maker Roles

- Country Manager.
- Managing Director.
- CFO or Finance Director.
- Head of Legal.
- Compliance Officer.
- Corporate Secretary.
- Founder or CEO.
- Investment or Expansion Lead.

## First Working Session

1. Add 15 telecom, fintech, and software prospects.
2. Find two decision-makers per company on LinkedIn.
3. Mark records as `Ready to Contact`.
4. Send the first 10 tailored messages using `outreach-playbook.md`.
5. Add follow-up dates three business days after the first message.
